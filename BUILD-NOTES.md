## Design identity — and why
- **Two registers, one page.** The host business sits in a deliberately quiet neutral register (warm paper #FAFAF8, ink #26232B, muted grey-violet text) so the product — the chat widget — is the visual star. The widget and every add-on signal (eyebrows, prices, step numbers, links, focus) carry the single accent: **violet #6D28D9** (dark #571CB0, soft #EFE9FB).
- **Why violet:** the ladder check — gift basket terracotta/emerald, restaurant brick, MTS lime, salon blush/bronze, clinic petrol, real-estate copper, booking indigo/rose/teal, LMS academic green, landing indigo/burnt-orange, travel plum/gold, directory teal-blue. Violet is the one hue family no predecessor used as its primary (travel's plum is a dark ink ground, not an accent). Violet also reads "AI product" to a prospect without a single claim being made — the honesty labels do the correcting.
- **Type:** Sora (display) + Inter (body/UI) — a product-showcase grotesque pairing, distinct from every predecessor's pairing.
- **Host business:** Pearl & Vale Dental Studio, Gulberg, Lahore — invented for this build. A dental studio fits the FAQ shape perfectly (prices per treatment, hours, parking, cancellation policy) and its Sunday closure lands exactly on the fixed demo clock (Sunday 22 Nov 2026, verified) — the boundary is visible on the hours table's highlighted row and in the bot's hours answer (lesson 41: put the boundary in the data).

## The honesty architecture (the brief's critical rule)
- The bot is **rule-based** and says so in four places: the panel header (permanent), the first greeting, the showcase lede (the exact mandated label: "Demo: rule-based FAQ bot — a production add-on connects to an AI model + the business's own data"), and the "What it is not" panel. The battery asserts the string "AI-powered / real AI / powered by AI" appears nowhere.
- **Answers cannot drift:** every answer is generated by `ANSWERS` builders in js/logic.js from the single `BIZ` data object — and the page's own services grid and hours table are rendered from that same object. The battery asserts answer content equals data values for every service price, every hours line, address, parking, phone, WhatsApp, email, offer and cancellation text.
- **Matching is stated, not implied:** keyword scoring (exact phrase 3 pts, weighted keywords, threshold 3) with a published priority rule — score first, ties by intent order, human help first. The battery proves both halves: a tie resolves to human; a higher score beats priority.
- **Off-scope is a designed answer,** not an error: the fallback names its scope ("services, hours and bookings"), offers chips, callback and WhatsApp. Five off-scope probes (weather, pizza, jokes, geography, and a medical-emergency question) all land on it.
- **Lead capture is a local stub** and says so at the point of capture (the confirmation message itself states the request "was written to a local log in your browser only — nothing was sent anywhere") and on the leads page. The battery asserts zero fetch/XHR/beacon/analytics and that the only external references are Google Fonts and the wa.me hand-off link.

## Defects found by reading captures, fixed, re-captured (disclosed)
1. **Chat input placeholder clipped mid-word** at panel width ("…hours, book" cut) — shortened to "Ask about prices, hours…" on both pages; re-captured (lesson 7).
2. **Dead launcher in the no-JS set** — the widget is JS-only, yet its launcher rendered (inert) with JS disabled. Fixed with an `html.js` gate (inline head script marks the class; widget hidden without it, noscript note explains). First attempt failed silently — the base launcher rule's own `display:inline-flex` out-cascaded the gate; found by re-reading the no-JS crop, fixed by removing the display from the base rule, re-verified by eye. (Lesson for #13: a visibility gate must be checked against every display declaration in the component's own rules, not just added beside them.)
3. Keyboard-walk method gap (not a site defect): the first walk Tabbed out of the non-modal panel before recording its controls; the walk was re-run walking Shift+Tab from the input so every widget control (Close, scrollable message list, all six chips, WhatsApp, Clear, Send) is recorded with visible focus.
